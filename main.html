BUGATTI

From the timeless automotive designs of Jean Bugatti to the masterful sculptures of Rembrandt Bugatti, the pursuit of singular artistry has defined the BUGATTI brand since its inception. The BUGATTI Programme Solitaire is committed to reviving the legacy of this tradition, redefining the art of coachbuilding that has been woven into BUGATTI’s DNA for over a century. It offers truly bespoke, one-of-one hypercars, brought to life through a deeply personal and meticulously curated collaboration between the brand and its future owner.
